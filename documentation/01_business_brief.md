# Business Brief

## Situation
An online toy store sold four products between 2012 and 2015 and ran paid and organic traffic campaigns. Growth has slowed, and marketing and merchandising want to know where to spend the next budget and what to fix first.

## Stakeholders and decisions

| Stakeholder | Decision | What they need from the analysis |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing manager | Where to spend paid search budget | Conversion and revenue after refunds, by channel |
| Website / UX lead | Which page to fix first | Drop-off at each step of the purchase path, by device |
| Merchandising lead | Which products to promote or bundle | Revenue, margin and refund rate by product |
| Finance partner | Whether refunds put revenue at risk | Refund rate and net revenue |

## Business questions

| # | Question | Decision it supports | What would change the decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Where do we lose the most visitors on the path to purchase? | Which page to fix first | A large drop at a step with high traffic |
| Q2 | Which channels bring in revenue that survives refunds? | Where to put the next marketing dollar | A channel with good conversion but high refunds |
| Q3 | Does device change conversion enough to act on? | Whether to prioritise mobile fixes | A large, persistent gap on the device with most sessions |
| Q4 | Did product launches add revenue, or move existing sales? | Whether to launch more products | Total revenue rising after a launch, not just the new product's share |
| Q5 | Are refunds concentrated in one product, month or channel? | Whether to investigate quality or expectations | A refund rate well above other products |
| Q6 | Do repeat customers behave differently from new ones? | Retention or acquisition spend | Repeat sessions converting much better |
| Q7 | Does ordering more than one item lift order value? | Whether to push bundles or add-ons | A clear lift in average order value |

## Scope
- **In:** sessions, page views, orders, items, refunds, products, UTM channels and devices, January 2012 to March 2015.
- **Out:** customer profit, inventory, shipping cost, and causes. The data shows association, not cause.

## Constraints
- No acquisition cost, so return on ad spend is not calculated; revenue and margin are the proxies.
- Partial months at the start (March 2012) and end (March 2015) are reported, but not used to claim trends.
